Data and method

Forecast vintage. The archive contains individual NOAA Global Forecast System deterministic 00 UTC model cycles on a 0.5° grid. A run label is its initialization time. Public availability follows model computation and dissemination. This project is a historical snapshot. New runs appear only after an explicit archive rebuild; it does not update automatically.

Identical dates. Each run contains nine complete UTC dates, beginning the day after initialization. Comparisons take the intersection of valid dates. The earlier-only dates that drop off and later-only dates that enter the forecast are excluded.

From temperature to degree days. For each 2020 Census county population center, the nearest GFS grid cell supplies 2 m air temperature at 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC. Their arithmetic mean gives a four-sample daily temperature in °F. Heating degree days equal max(65 − mean, 0). Cooling degree days equal max(mean − 65, 0). Both use a 65°F base. State and national averages weight those county values by 2020 resident population. The national series covers the contiguous 48 states and DC.

Interpretation. An increase in HDD indicates cooler conditions relative to the 65°F base. An increase in CDD indicates warmer conditions above that base. These are weather-exposure indicators. Gas consumption in Bcf would require a separate demand model. Grid resolution, UTC sampling and fixed population weights affect the estimates.
